Featured Project

Remote Gaming Architecture: Bypassing LAN Limitations

Apollo & Artemis Tailscale (Mesh Networking) ESP32 Wake-on-LAN

What started as a simple idea—wanting to play my desktop PC games from a beach house on my Retroid Pocket 5—turned into a deep dive into host-client streaming software. To achieve ultra-low latency without relying on cloud subscriptions, I built a self-hosted ecosystem. The setup uses Apollo and Artemis for native-resolution streaming, Tailscale to establish a secure private mesh network, and an ESP32 microchip programmed to route Wake-on-LAN magic packets locally to my sleeping PC from anywhere in the world.
